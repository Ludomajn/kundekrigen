**One-sentence pitch:** A Worms-like turn-based artillery game where angry clinic
customers fight each other with the clinic's own IT: toner cannons, data-leak bombs,
phishing mines and quarterly invoices.

## Core loop
- Take a turn (45 s, configurable 15–60): walk, aim, charge power, fire a weapon.
- Wind and weather bend the shot; terrain is destroyed, so the map changes every turn.
- Pick up supply crates (weapons, phones, pills), then end the turn; shots and chain
  reactions resolve before the next clinic plays.

## Player goal & fail state — what "working" looks like
- Win: be the last clinic with a living customer. A customer dies at 0 patience,
  by drowning, or by falling off the map.
- A match ends at a winner; after 30 min sudden death starts and the water rises
  every turn, so every match reaches a result.

## MVP — what must exist to be the game
*(Core per author: turn-based artillery + destructible terrain. All items below are BUILT.)*
1. Turn machine: 45 s turns, retreat after firing, 30 min match, then sudden death.
2. Destructible terrain: 5120×1792 pixel mask, rule-built maps, fort map as default.
3. Arsenal: 15 weapons + 2 meta choices (data-driven in `sim/weapons.js`), limited
   ammo, supply crates.
4. Wind and weather physics acting on shots (`VEJR_FYSIK` in `sim/physics.js`).
5. Hit zone and damage: full hit = full damage ×1.3; patience bars with delayed damage.
6. Hotseat play: 2–4 clinics, up to 12 customers.

Shipped, but not part of the MVP definition: networked rooms and lobby, 13 random
events (from round 3), real-time hazards (from round 2), character select, film intro.

## Out of scope — not building this
- Ninja rope (README: "biggest time sink in any Worms clone").
- AI opponents (an absent player's customers are skipped, not killed).
- Cheat protection, accounts/login, server-side persistence (rooms die on restart).
- Mobile/touch; desktop 16:9 only.
- Collapse of detached terrain (Worms doesn't do it either).
